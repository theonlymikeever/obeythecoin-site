obey the coin privacy policy

last updated: august 28, 2026

the short version: everything stays on your device. no accounts, no tracking, no servers, no ads.

what we collect

nothing. obey the coin collects no personal information, no usage data, no analytics, and no identifiers. the app never connects to the network.

what stays on your device

your flip statistics (lifetime flips, sessions, streaks, last flip date), your chosen coin face, and your custom flip buttons are stored locally on your device so the app and its widget can share them. they never leave your device, and deleting the app deletes them.

third parties

there are none. the app contains no third-party code, no advertising SDKs, and no analytics frameworks.

children

the app collects no data from anyone, children included.

changes

if this policy ever changes, the new version will be posted at this address with an updated date.

contact

questions? email support@mikekerslake.com.
